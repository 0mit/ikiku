The spine

One tree, two layers, and a promotion path

Everything you asked for — generalize hard, never lose the specific, don't get locked into building features — is one mechanism, and it is the mechanism daftar already uses: a standard layer plus per-tenant overlays, held as data, with one interpreter.

  • Standard layer. One versioned tree of competencies, hygiene protocols and procedure steps, owned by the platform's technical masters. Portable across every business. This is Tier-0.
  • Overlay. A business declares only its deltas — its own recipes, its own order of work, its own tolerances. An overlay never redefines the standard, it points at a node and adds to it.
  • Candidates. Anything a business says that does not resolve into the tree is stored intact as a candidate node, never discarded and never bent into a term that nearly fits.
  • Promotion. A candidate that recurs across businesses above a threshold is proposed to a master, who promotes it into the standard with a version bump. That is the whole generalization engine, and it runs on real usage rather than on a taxonomy meeting.
Why this answers "don't tie us to developing features": a new sector — a bakery, a hotel, a workshop — is a new subtree of data, not a new module. The gate that validates the tree is generic and names no term. You add verticals by writing rows.

The employment question is settled, and it simplified things. Resources are employed by the businesses they are placed with; iKiKu records, verifies and — through the dispute procedure — acts as their union. So there is no employment contract, no payroll and no hr.employee in this codebase, and no labour-supply licensing exposure. employer_business_id on every booking names who the employer actually is.

Article 7 as data

Three visibility classes, one interpreter

Ratified today: the professional record is public, the calendar is not. This is not per-field code — it is a policy table that the portal serializer reads, the same way the daftar gate reads its vocabulary.

ClassWho sees itWhat lives there
publicAnyone, no loginSkills and levels, certifications, verified work history at business granularity, ratings with their full chain, court rulings, and the fact of a booking: this person is committed, these dates, this province.

The publication rule for a booking

  • On confirmation, publish immediately: «این نیرو از ۱۲ تا ۲۰ مرداد ۱۴۰۵ در استان فارس متعهد است» — committed, dates, province. No venue, no hours.
  • The venue becomes public when the shift starts — or earlier if both parties opt in.
  • Cancellation is public the moment it happens. This is where the enforcement actually lives: a commitment cannot be quietly withdrawn, by either side.
National ID is never stored. Store a salted hash for deduplication and the verification verdict — not the number, and never a scan of the card. A verified national-ID database of named workers is a honeypot, and in this country it is the single asset most worth stealing from you.

Matching

Three months out, and checked three times before anyone travels

A business declares demand for a window starting roughly a quarter ahead; the matcher searches resources whose declared availability covers it. The MVP horizon is [today + 3mo, today + 6mo].

draftDemandPosition, seats, province, Jalali window, season tag, must-have vs nice-to-have competencies.
proposedRanked setCandidates with their score components stored on the record.
confirmedBookingHuman-validated on both sides. Published the same second.
T-14d · T-3d · T-24hRe-checkA task per checkpoint to the call centre: is this person still on schedule?
at_risk → replacedReplacementA failed check reopens the ranked pool. The original booking is never deleted — it is closed with a reason, publicly.

The ranking must be readable, which rules out a model

The manifest says no hidden formula: «هر عددی که نشان می‌دهیم، راهِ رسیدن به آن هم پیداست». So the MVP ranker is additive with visible weights, and every proposal stores its own components — competency match, verified history depth, geographic distance, season load, standing. A learned ranker cannot satisfy that sentence and should not be attempted later either without changing the manifest first.

Verification

Assertions and verdicts, never a bare star

  • ikiku.assertion — a claim about someone: subject, claim type (a spec node or a résumé line), value, who asserted it, when, and by what method — self, co-worker, employer, academy, platform, document.
  • ikiku.verification — a verdict on one assertion: verifier, confirm / dispute / cannot-say, evidence, and the verifier's own standing at the time they said it, frozen.
  • Strength is derived, never stored as a number someone can nudge. The profile shows the chain, not a score alone.
  • dispute_id exists from day one, even though the court is phase 2. A rating that cannot point at its defence is a rating that violates بند ۴, and adding the column later means backfilling every row that was never defensible.
Anti-Sybil, cheaply and explainably: a verification's weight comes from the verifier's own verified standing. Two accounts that only verify each other converge toward zero weight without anyone being banned, and the rule fits in one sentence on a public page — which the manifest requires it to.

Hint and steer

Ask in their language, store in ours

Three rules, applied identically on the business side, the resource side and the internal side.

  • Never a bare free-text field. Every open question is paired with a resolution step: the person types freely, the system proposes standard nodes, the person confirms.
  • The remainder is kept. Whatever does not resolve becomes a candidate node attached to the record, verbatim. Nothing is dropped to make the form validate.
  • One question per screen, six screens maximum. These users are on phones, often mid-shift.

Worked example, business side: the owner types «یکی که ظرف بشوره و شیفت شب وایسه». The wizard proposes kitchen/dishwashing and shift/night, shows them in Persian with plain descriptions, and asks for confirmation. What it cannot resolve — a house term, an unusual tolerance — is stored as a candidate and reaches a master's promotion queue if three other businesses say something close to it.

Jalali

An axis, not a display setting

  • Store Gregorian and UTC. Always. Convert only at the boundary — portal rendering and portal input. A Jalali string in a database column is a defect.
  • Vendor the converter. It is about sixty lines of arithmetic. A pip dependency that has to be fetched through a filtered network is a build that fails at the worst moment.
  • ikiku.season per province, materialized into concrete date ranges per year: Nowruz (1–13 Farvardin), the summer peak, Ramadan, Muharram, Arbaeen. Demand in Kish in Dey is not demand in Tabriz in Dey, and the platform has to know that before it ranks anyone.
  • Ramadan and Muharram drift. They are Hijri, roughly eleven days earlier each Gregorian year, and Iran's determination is observational. Compute a tabular Hijri date, then let a human override it per year. Do not pretend the arithmetic is authoritative.
  • Backoffice may stay Gregorian for staff. The two Persian portals and the public site are Jalali throughout, with a Jalali picker.

Deployment: what Iran actually permits

These are not preferences. Each one has already cost somebody a day.

  • Not Odoo.sh, not GCP. Your own ledger records the measurement: every Odoo.sh build host resolves to 35.241.236.166 and times out on both 22 and 443, while odoo.com answers in 0.20s. The platform address is blocked, not your egress. iKiKu is self-hosted, in-country or on a provider that answers from Iran.
  • Self-host the fonts. Vazirmatn is SIL OFL — ship it with the assets. No production page should depend on fonts.googleapis.com resolving. The two documents you are reading do use it, because they are hosted outside; the product must not.
  • Audit Community for CDN references before launch — themes and map widgets reach for Google endpoints, and each one is a page that half-loads for an Iranian user with no error anyone will report.
  • Video is not YouTube. website_slides embeds YouTube and Google Drive by default; both are unreliable or blocked here. Self-host, or embed Aparat and extend the slide type.
  • OTP through an Iranian SMS gateway. Phone is your establishing anchor, so this is on the critical path for signup, not an integration for later.
The manifest constrains hosting too. بند ۷ says the restricted class holds national IDs and future shift locations. That data should not sit on infrastructure whose operator can be compelled without you knowing. Where it lands is a founding decision, not an ops ticket.

Phases

My assumed boundary, since you have not set one. Move it and I will redraw.

01

The loop that proves the idea

Spec tree and hint-and-steer intake. Both Persian portals. The verification ledger and the public professional record. Availability, matching at the three-month horizon, publicly acknowledged bookings, and the pre-shift re-check with replacement. Public site listing open positions and public profiles.

One province, one city, one academy partner. This is enough to test whether businesses trust a verified stranger — which is the only question that matters.

02

What makes it worth staying for

Knowledge base and learning on website_slides + survey. Academy portal, cohorts, certification. The internal court with its full procedure. The call centre at scale. Season and demand analytics per province.

03

Non-negotiables that must ship in phase 1 anyway

Each of these is cheap now and either expensive or impossible later: the visibility classes, the dispute_id column, the Jalali axis, the stored score components, and phone-based identity. Retrofitting any one of them means rewriting history you have already published — and the manifest forbids rewriting published history.

Built

Phase 1 is written, and not yet installed anywhere

Six modules, 23 models, about 2,100 lines of Python and 1,900 of XML, all depending only on LGPL-3 Odoo. Every module carries 'license': 'AGPL-3'.

  • The calendar is verified. jalali.py is vendored and checked against five known dates including Nowruz 1405 and 22 Bahman 1357, with a round-trip assertion each way. It caught a real error: both published documents were dated ۱۳ شهریور ۱۴۰۴ when today is ۱۲ شهریور ۱۴۰۵ — wrong day and wrong year. Corrected.
  • What was NOT possible here. This machine has no PostgreSQL and no psycopg2 or werkzeug, so Odoo was never booted and the modules have never been installed. What ran was tools/validate.py — manifest files exist, ACLs resolve to declared models, every view field exists on the model of the subview it sits in, no model lacks an ACL — plus compileall and an XML parse of all 25 files. 0 errors, 0 warnings. That is not the same as a green install, and the first -i ikiku_portal will find things the checker cannot.
  • Fonts are not in the tree. tools/fetch_fonts.sh pulls Vazirmatn from GitHub; it has not been run, because production must not depend on a CDN answering from inside Iran and this environment should not be the one to fetch it.
  • Set ikiku.nid_salt before any signup. Without it, hash_national_id raises rather than falling back — a national ID must never be stored under a guessable salt, and failing loudly is the only safe default.
