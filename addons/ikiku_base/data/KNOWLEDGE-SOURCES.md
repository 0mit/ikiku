# The international classifications in this directory

`ikiku.knowledge.class.csv` and `ikiku.knowledge.scheme.csv` hold two published classifications. Each keeps
its own terms, separate from the AGPL-3.0 code of this repository. The notice each licence or permission
requires is also data (`notice`, `notice_en` in `ikiku.knowledge.scheme.csv`), and every public page that
shows these titles displays it.

## ISCED-F 2013 (rows `kc_isced_*`)

- **Source:** UNESCO Institute for Statistics, *ISCED Fields of Education and Training 2013 (ISCED-F 2013)*,
  doi:10.15220/978-92-9189-179-5-en.
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO. The rows are an adaptation: codes and English titles as published, and
  Persian titles added by iKiKu. The adaptation is released under the same licence, CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO.
- **Attribution:** "Source: UNESCO Institute for Statistics, ISCED-F 2013. Modified: Persian titles added."

## ISCO-08 (rows `kc_isco_*`)

- **Source:** International Labour Organization, *International Standard Classification of Occupations:
  ISCO-08*, Geneva, 2012: codes and English titles only. Definitions and task descriptions are not
  reproduced.
- **Copyright:** © International Labour Organization, 2012. The ILO's pre-2023 works carry no open licence.
- **Persian titles:** a translation made by iKiKu, with AI assistance. It is not an official translation
  and is not the Statistical Centre of Iran's national classification. **Permission to publish it has been
  requested from the ILO (rights@ilo.org).** If the ILO declines, the Persian titles are removed and the
  English titles take their place.
- **Required notice:** "This translation has not been prepared, reviewed or endorsed by the ILO and should
  not be considered an official ILO translation. The ILO disclaims all responsibility for its content and
  accuracy. Responsibility rests solely with the authors of the translation."
